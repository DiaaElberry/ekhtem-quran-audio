Ekhtem AlQur'an — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Android and iOS apps (package / bundle ID com.softsolvit.hq), published by Softsolvit.

Ekhtem AlQur'an is a Qur'an reading and listening app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no app server. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Reading position, bookmarks, khatmah plans, athkar counters and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Recitations you download are saved in the app's private storage. You can delete them in the app at any time.
  • On Android, your reading progress and settings may be included in your device's backup if you have Android backup turned on; downloaded audio is excluded. On iOS, downloaded audio is excluded from iCloud backup.

Location

If you tap "Use my location" or open the Qibla compass, the app asks for permission to read your device location once. It is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can instead choose a city or enter coordinates manually, and you can revoke the permission in your device settings.

Motion sensors

The Qibla compass reads the device compass and gyroscope while the compass screen is open. These readings are used only on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Prayer and khatmah reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service is used.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only when you download or stream a recitation. The audio files are served from GitHub (github.com and GitHub's file servers). As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app sends no identifiers or personal information with these requests.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because all data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer using the email address shown on the app's Google Play or App Store page.

اختم القرآن — سياسة الخصوصية

سارية من 30 أكتوبر 2026 · تنطبق على تطبيقي أندرويد وiOS (المعرّف com.softsolvit.hq)، من نشر Softsolvit.

اختم القرآن تطبيق لقراءة القرآن الكريم والاستماع إليه. لا يوجد فيه حسابات ولا إعلانات ولا أدوات تحليل ولا خادم خاص بالتطبيق. نحن لا نجمع أي بيانات شخصية ولا نخزّنها ولا نبيعها ولا نشاركها.

البيانات المحفوظة على جهازك

  • موضع القراءة والعلامات وخطط الختمة وعدّادات الأذكار والإعدادات تُحفظ في التخزين الخاص بالتطبيق على جهازك فقط.
  • التلاوات التي تنزّلها تُحفظ في التخزين الخاص بالتطبيق، ويمكنك حذفها من داخل التطبيق في أي وقت.
  • على أندرويد قد يُضمَّن تقدّمك في القراءة وإعداداتك في النسخ الاحتياطي لجهازك إذا كان مفعّلًا، ولا تُضمَّن التلاوات المنزّلة. وعلى iOS تُستثنى التلاوات المنزّلة من النسخ الاحتياطي على iCloud.
